# SEO and launch checklist

## Before launch

- [ ] Confirm business name, telephone and address.
- [ ] Confirm opening hours.
- [ ] Confirm which grooming services are actually offered.
- [ ] Confirm whether the business wants to publish any prices.
- [ ] Obtain permission for customer dog photographs.
- [ ] Complete and review the privacy policy.
- [ ] Check all phone links on mobile.
- [ ] Check every page title and meta description.
- [ ] Validate PetGroomer JSON-LD using Google's Rich Results Test / Schema Markup Validator.
- [ ] Check the map pin and directions.
- [ ] Add the domain and verify HTTPS.

## Google

- [ ] Set up or verify Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Ensure business name, address and phone match the website exactly.
- [ ] Add the website URL to the Business Profile.
- [ ] Add genuine photographs.
- [ ] Ask customers for honest reviews without incentives.
- [ ] Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
- [ ] Submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Inspect the homepage and key service/contact pages.

## Search and AI visibility

- [ ] Make service information and FAQs crawlable in page HTML.
- [ ] Keep address, phone and business identity consistent.
- [ ] Use clear headings and natural local wording.
- [ ] Link service, FAQ, about and contact pages together.
- [ ] Keep `robots.txt` and sitemap accessible.
- [ ] Consider a concise `llms.txt` once final service and business details are confirmed.

Search rankings and AI citations cannot be guaranteed. These steps provide accurate, crawlable information and a clear local business entity.
